# antislop visual audit 002, 2026-09-17

Scope: the visual UI of `src/` (palette, contrast, layout, radius, motion, icons,
keyboard) and `src/index.css`. Copy was covered by audit-001 and is not repeated.

Mode: AFTER. Nothing has been changed. Approve numbers to fix.

Method: read every component and the stylesheet, then computed contrast ratios
and mobile cell widths from the actual token values rather than eyeballing.

## Summary

The structure is in better shape than the copy audit suggested it might be. The
five-tab nav is honest (every tab has a real panel, R-24 passes), empty states
exist throughout (R-27 passes), there are no em or en dashes, no `outline: none`,
and `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected.

Two real problems stand out.

**The hero gradient fails contrast in four of six phases.** This is the headline
finding: the app's most prominent element, on every launch, with white text over
gradients whose lightest stop gives 1.52:1. R-25 explicitly forbids white text on
a gradient that is light in some areas, and this is exactly that.

**Light mode fails AA for small muted text.** `--muted` on `--card` is 3.44:1
against a 4.5:1 requirement, and it is used at 11 to 13px in badges, notes, and
labels across the app. Dark mode passes comfortably.

Also: no `DESIGN.md`, so R-37 applies to everything here.

## Findings

### F-01 — Hero gradient fails contrast in 4 of 6 phases (HIGH, R-25)

Computed white text against each gradient stop:

| phase | top | mid | bottom | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| period | 2.26 | 3.91 | 6.50 | FAIL |
| fertile | 1.59 | 3.16 | 6.47 | FAIL |
| ovulation | 1.86 | 3.43 | 8.05 | FAIL |
| pms | 1.52 | 2.95 | 5.68 | FAIL |
| neutral | 7.86 | 6.18 | 4.51 | pass |
| bc | 7.05 | 5.33 | 3.73 | pass |

The subtitle is worse: `rgba(255,255,255,.9)` over the top stop is 1.45:1 on pms
and 1.52:1 on fertile.

The title is 24px bold, so it needs 3:1 and the top stop fails that too on all
four. The subtitle is 14px, needs 4.5:1, and fails at every stop on all four.

Fix options, in order of preference:
1. Drop the top stop's lightness so the whole gradient passes 4.5:1 for the
   subtitle. Darkening four stops is a real change to the look.
2. Move the text block down, away from the lightest part of the radial. The
   gradient is `at 50% 0%`, so the top edge is always the lightest.
3. Give the subtitle a solid dark colour on light phases instead of white.

I would take 2 plus 1, but this is a visual decision and it is yours.

Files: `src/Home.tsx` (`GRAD`), `src/index.css` (`.hero-sub`, `.hero-meta`)

### F-02 — Light-mode muted text fails AA at 11 to 13px (HIGH, R-25)

| pair | ratio | needs | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--muted` on `--card` (light) | 3.44 | 4.5 | FAIL |
| `--muted` on `--bg` (light) | 3.23 | 4.5 | FAIL |
| `--rose` on `--card` (light) | 3.91 | 4.5 | FAIL |
| `--rose` on `--rose-soft` (light) | 3.56 | 4.5 | FAIL |
| `--green` on `--card` (light) | 3.16 | 4.5 | FAIL |
| `--green` on `--green-soft` (light) | 2.86 | 4.5 | FAIL |
| `--amber-line` on `--amber` (light) | 1.98 | 4.5 | FAIL |
| `--heart` on `--card` (light) | 3.53 | 4.5 | FAIL |

All of these render at 11 to 13px, where the 4.5:1 threshold applies: `.badge`
(11px), `.chance-note` (11px), `.ins-note` (11px), `.hint-inline` (12px),
`.day-info-label` (12px), `.sym-chip` (12px), `.chance-badge` (12px), `.muted`
(13px), `.update-sub` (12px). Dark mode passes every pair.

The amber pair at 1.98:1 is the worst: `.badge.risk-medium` uses `--amber-line`
on `--amber`, which is close to invisible.

Fix: darken the light-mode tokens. `--muted` needs roughly `#6b6b70` to clear
4.5:1 on both `--card` and `--bg`; `--green` and `--rose` need the same
treatment; `--amber-line` needs to move well away from `--amber`.

Files: `src/index.css` (`:root` tokens)

### F-03 — No design direction, so nothing here is a deliverable (HIGH, R-37)

There is no `DESIGN.md` and no dial declaration anywhere in the repo. R-37 says a
design built without direction is a draft, not a shippable result, and must carry
the honest default dials ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

The app is currently closer to ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2: six phase
gradients, an animated loading screen, staggered card entrances. So it is not
even the honest default; it is an undeclared middle.

Fix: either write `DESIGN.md` with your direction and declared dials, or label
the app a draft. I cannot write your direction for you without it being my taste
rather than yours, which R-37 warns against.

Files: new `DESIGN.md`

### F-04 — Only one of five sheets closes on Escape (MEDIUM, R-32)

`UpdateBanner` handles Escape. `LogSheet`, `DaySheet`, `BcPanel`, and `EcPanel`
do not, so the four most-used dialogs cannot be closed from the keyboard. All
five have `aria-modal="true"`, so they claim modal behaviour without the keyboard
contract that goes with it.

Fix: one shared hook (`useEscape(open, onClose)`) used by all five.

Files: `src/LogSheet.tsx`, `src/DaySheet.tsx`, `src/BcPanel.tsx`,
`src/EcPanel.tsx`

### F-05 — Calendar day cells overflow at 320px (MEDIUM, R-03)

`.dnum` is a fixed 38px inside a 7-column grid with 4px gaps.

```
320px viewport -> inner 288px, cell 37.7px, .dnum 38px -> OVERFLOW
360px viewport -> inner 328px, cell 43.4px, .dnum 38px -> ok
```

0.3px over, so it will not visibly break, but it is a fixed size in a fluid grid
and it is the first thing to go if the padding ever changes. The 44px tap-target
requirement is also not met at any width (38px).

Fix: `width: 100%; max-width: 44px; aspect-ratio: 1` so the cell is fluid and the
tap target reaches 44px where space allows.

Files: `src/index.css` (`.dnum`)

### F-06 — `--amber-line` on `--amber` is 1.98:1 (MEDIUM, R-25)

Called out separately from F-02 because it is the worst pair in the app and it
carries meaning: it is the "medium pregnancy chance" badge and the BC/EC warning
banner. A warning that cannot be read is worse than no warning.

Files: `src/index.css`, `src/ChanceCard.tsx`, `src/App.tsx`

### F-07 — Fourteen distinct radius values with no system (LOW, R-11)

`999px` (7 uses), `50%` (5), `12px` (5), `10px` (3), `22px` (2), then `var(--radius)`,
`20px`, `20px 20px 0 0`, `18px`, `9px`, `8px`, `4px`, `2px`, `0 0 28px 28px`.

R-11 requires radius consistent with a defined system and forbids pill-shaping
everything. Seven uses of `999px` is a lot, though each has a reason (badges,
chips, capsules, the logout pill). The problem is the long tail: `8px`, `9px`,
`10px`, and `12px` are four values doing one job.

Fix: collapse to a scale (4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / full) and use it everywhere. Low
priority because nothing looks broken today.

Files: `src/index.css`

### F-08 — Motion is undeclared and has no stated purpose (LOW, R-19)

Eight keyframes, twelve transitions, staggered delays on cards and list items.
Each animation is individually defensible: `rise` on cards gives a sense of
arrival, `pop` on onboarding gives feedback, `breathe`/`dotpulse` on the loading
screen signal progress. But R-19 requires the purpose written down and motion
consistent with a declared MOTION dial, and F-03 means there is no dial.

Not a defect on its own. It becomes one once F-03 is resolved: either the dial
says 2 and this is correct, or it says 1 and the stagger should go.

Files: `src/index.css`

## Passing checks (evidence, not assumption)

- **R-24**: all five tabs (`home`, `calendar`, `insights`, `history`, `settings`)
  have a real panel in `App.tsx`. No nav item points nowhere.
- **R-26**: no dead controls found. Every button has an `onClick` or is a submit.
- **R-27**: empty states exist for every data view (`insEmpty`, `nothing`,
  `notEnough`, `symHistoryEmpty`, `doseNone`, `sexNone`, `dayNoNote`,
  `dayNoSymptoms`), plus a loading screen and error states.
- **R-02**: no `—`, `–`, or `…` in rendered strings. Re-verified after audit-001.
- **R-32 (partial)**: no `outline: none` anywhere; `.btn`, `.tab`, `.nav-btn`,
  `.seg-btn`, `.pw-toggle`, `.link` all have `:focus-visible` rings.
- **R-34**: dark mode passes every contrast pair checked. It is not a broken mode.
- **R-19 (partial)**: `prefers-reduced-motion` disables all animation.
- **R-04**: `Icon.tsx` is hand-rolled on a 24x24 / 1.8 stroke grid with
  `currentColor`, not a Lucide clone, and the home icon is a heart matching the
  sex-log marker. Relevance is stated in the file header.
- **R-01 (partial)**: the six phase gradients are not the blue-purple default.
  They encode cycle phase, which is a real hierarchy job. Their problem is
  contrast (F-01), not purpose.
